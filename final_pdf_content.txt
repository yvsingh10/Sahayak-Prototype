SAHAYAK
A PROTOTYPE FOR PUBLIC PROBLEM REPORTING AND RESOLUTION SYSTEM

--- PAGE 2 ---
UNDERSTANDING THE TOPIC
ΓÇó SAHAYAK is a digital prototype designed to help citizens report public and social problems.
ΓÇó It creates a direct communication channel between citizens and government authorities.
ΓÇó The system focuses on transparency, accountability, and faster governance.

--- PAGE 3 ---
BACKGROUND
ΓÇó In many areas, public problems remain unresolved due to poor communication.
ΓÇó Citizens do not have a single trusted platform to report issues.
ΓÇó Lack of monitoring and accountability causes delays.

--- PAGE 4 ---
PROBLEM DEFINITION
ΓÇó Public issues such as road damage, pollution, sanitation, and local crime are often ignored.
ΓÇó Complaints are delayed due to middlemen and unclear authority responsibility.
ΓÇó There is no fixed time for approval or priority handling of problems.

--- PAGE 5 ---
KEY ISSUES IDENTIFIED
NO TRANSPARENCY
No transparency in how public problems are handled once 
reported.
NO PRIORITY SYSTEM
No priority-based system to distinguish urgent issues from 
minor ones.
DELAYED APPROVAL
Delayed approval of complaints due to unclear responsibility.
STATUS UNAWARENESS
Citizens remain unaware of the current status of their 
problem.

--- PAGE 6 ---
NEED OF SAHAYAK
ΓÇó To provide a single digital platform for public problem reporting.
ΓÇó To ensure approval of problems within 48 hours.
ΓÇó To prioritize problems based on public support.
ΓÇó To improve trust between citizens and government.

--- PAGE 7 ---
OBJECTIVES OF SAHAYAK
ΓÇó Provide a direct platform for citizens to report public problems.
ΓÇó Ensure problem approval within 48 hours.
ΓÇó Prioritize problems based on severity and public support.
ΓÇó Increase transparency and accountability in governance.
ΓÇó Enable community participation in problem resolution.

--- PAGE 8 ---
KEY FEATURES
PUBLIC ISSUE REPORTING
Report roads, pollution, local crime, 
and sanitation issues.
COMMUNITY VISIBILITY
Reshare and upvote system to boost 
community-backed problems.
PRIORITY MANAGEMENT
Problems classified as high, medium, 
or low priority.
TIME-BOUND APPROVAL
Every problem is reviewed and 
approved within 48 hours.
ESCALATION SYSTEM
Delayed issues are automatically 
escalated for action.
AUTHORITY ASSIGNMENT
Direct assignment to municipal, 
police, or local leaders.

--- PAGE 9 ---
WORKING OF SAHAYAK PROTOTYPE
ΓÇó Citizen submits a problem with description, image, and location.
ΓÇó Problem enters 'Pending Approval' state (max 48 hours).
ΓÇó Authority approves and assigns priority based on severity and community support.
ΓÇó Approved problem receives status updates visible to the public.
ΓÇó High-priority issues are resolved first; delays are escalated.
ΓÇó Community can reshare/upvote problems to increase priority.

--- PAGE 10 ---
BENEFITS OF SAHAYAK
ΓÇó Faster acknowledgment and resolution of public problems.
ΓÇó Transparency in problem reporting and handling.
ΓÇó Accountability of government authorities.
ΓÇó Community participation in prioritizing issues.
ΓÇó Trust between citizens and government improves.
