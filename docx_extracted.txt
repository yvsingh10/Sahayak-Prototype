On-Slide Highlights:

Real-time civic problem reporting
Core pillars: Transparency, Accountability, and Speed
Spoken Script (What to say):
"To understand what SAHAYAK is: it is a centralized digital bridge connecting common citizens directly with local authorities."

Slide 3: Background
On-Slide Highlights:
Unresolved everyday civic problems (roads, sanitation, drainage)
Absence of a single trusted reporting channel
Lack of status monitoring leading to forgotten complaints
Spoken Script (What to say):
"Looking at the background of our civic ecosystem, daily issues like potholes, sewage leaks, and garbage overflow often persist for weeks or months."
"Citizens lack a single, reliable point of contact and are forced to jump between helplines, local offices, or social media posts."
"Because there is no unified tracking mechanism, most complaints slip through the cracks and are eventually forgotten."
Delivery Tip: 
(~35 seconds)
 Emphasize the shared frustration every citizen 
experiences
 with unresolved complaints.
Slide 4: Problem Definition
On-Slide Highlights:
Critical public issues frequently neglected
Unclear departmental ownership & intermediary delays
No standard SLAs (Service Level Agreements) for reviews
Spoken Script (What to say):
"We have defined three core problem dimensions:"
"First, crucial public safety and sanitation complaints are easily overlooked due to noise and lack of organization."
"Second, citizens face heavy delays because departmental boundaries are blurred, passing responsibility from one desk to another."
"Third, there is no fixed timeline or SLA holding any official accountable to acknowledge or prioritize these issues."
Delivery Tip: 
(~35 seconds)
 Clearly identify the operational bottlenecks before presenting your solution.
Slide 5: Key Issues Identified
On-Slide Highlights:
Zero Transparency: Citizens don't know who is handling the complaint.
No Priority Logic: Urgent safety risks are treated the same as minor issues.
Delayed Approvals: Unclear ownership stalls action.
Status Blindness: No real-time updates for the complainant.
Spoken Script (What to say):
"Breaking down the current breakdown into four specific flaws:"
"1. No Transparency: Once a citizen 
complains
, the case enters a black box."
"2. No Priority Mechanism: A hazardous open manhole is queued alongside routine maintenance."
"3. Delayed Approvals: Bureaucratic bottlenecks stall initial verification."
"4. Status Unawareness: Citizens are left guessing whether any action has even begun."
Delivery Tip: 
(~40 seconds)
 Use hand gestures or point directly to the 4 quadrants on your slide.
Slide 6: Need for SAHAYAK
On-Slide Highlights:
Unified single-window reporting system
Mandatory 48-Hour Approval Window
Public-backed priority mechanism
Rebuilding citizen-government trust
Spoken Script (What to say):
"This is precisely why SAHAYAK is needed."
"It replaces scattered informal complaints with one structured, verified digital interface."
"Critically, it enforces a strict 48-hour approval SLA, guaranteeing that every problem is acknowledged quickly."
"It also introduces community-backed prioritization, ensuring the most pressing public issues get addressed first."
